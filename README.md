   # Truelow

   A gaming PC optimizer for Windows 10 and 11 that shows its work.

   - **Performance Lab:** record a game before and after a change, and see FPS, 1% lows, frame times and hitches compared, with a check that tells real gains from run-to-run noise.
   - **Setup audit:** finds the costly mistakes Windows never mentions: monitor plugged into the motherboard, Resizable BAR off, GPU short on PCIe lanes, single-channel or slow memory, games on a hard drive.
   - **Stutter diagnostics:** what your PC was doing at each hitch.
   - **Reversible tweaks:** each one shows how strong the evidence behind it is. Originals are backed up, every change is verified, and you can undo any tweak on its own.
   - **Fix my problem**, temperature monitor, health checks, background app inspector, game profiles and session summaries.
